Asset Purchase Decisions

When Equipment Financing May Support the Business

A strong request explains why this asset, why now, and how it supports the operation.

  1. 01

    Existing Equipment Limits Capacity

    A bottleneck can restrict output even when customer demand is available.

  2. 02

    Downtime Is Becoming Expensive

    Unreliable machinery may cost more in delays, repairs, and lost work.

  3. 03

    A New Service Needs Specialized Tools

    The right equipment can create a practical path into an added revenue stream.

  4. 04

    Technology No Longer Integrates

    Outdated systems can slow workflows and create avoidable manual work.

  5. 05

    Cash Is Needed Elsewhere

    Some owners compare financing with paying the full purchase from operating reserves.

Evaluate the Asset

Four Steps Before Financing Business Equipment

Compare the asset's business value with every obligation attached to the funding.

  1. 01

    Specify the Equipment

    Gather a current vendor quote and document the intended business use.

  2. 02

    Estimate Business Impact

    Consider capacity, savings, reliability, and likely useful life in the operation.

  3. 03

    Review Provider Terms

    Compare total obligation, payment structure, fees, and ownership or security provisions.

  4. 04

    Plan Delivery and Setup

    Account for installation, training, insurance, and downtime around the transition.
